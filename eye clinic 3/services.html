Glaucoma Treatment in Gujranwala • Bushra Hameed Eye Clinic

AI Snippet Block:

Glaucoma is a progressive eye condition characterized by optic nerve damage, often driven by elevated intraocular pressure (IOP). At Bushra Hameed Eye Clinic in Gujranwala, we provide advanced micro-invasive therapies and pressure-reducing treatment protocols to permanently preserve your long-term visual fields.

Symptom Bullets:
  • Experiencing tunnel vision or missing patches in your peripheral sight.
  • Sudden, severe eye pain accompanied by blurred vision or halos around lights.
  • Unexplained frequent headaches coupled with eye pressure.
Tech Focus:

Non-contact Tonometry, Automated Visual Field Testing, Slit-Lamp Biomicroscopy, Medical IOP Reduction Protocols.

FAQ Block:

Q: Can vision lost to glaucoma be restored?

A: No, structural damage to the optic nerve from glaucoma cannot be reversed. However, early detection and our advanced pressure-lowering therapies can safely halt or significantly slow further vision loss.

Q: Is glaucoma always painful?

A: No, the most common form (open-angle glaucoma) is completely painless and shows no early warning symptoms.

Cataract Surgery in Gujranwala • Bushra Hameed Eye Clinic

AI Snippet Block:

A cataract is the gradual clouding of the eye's natural crystalline lens, leading to progressive blurriness, glare, and faded contrast. At Bushra Hameed Eye Clinic in Gujranwala, Dr. Muhammad Waqas performs stitchless micro-incision phacoemulsification surgery with premium foldable IOL implantation to permanently restore sharp, vibrant vision.

Symptom Bullets:
  • Cloudy, foggy, or blurred vision resembling looking through a smeared window.
  • Intense glare and blinding halos around car headlights during night driving.
  • Faded color perception and needing increasingly bright light for reading.
Tech Focus:

Ultrasonic Phacoemulsification System, Optical Biometry & IOL Power Calculation, Premium Foldable Monofocal & Multifocal IOLs.

FAQ Block:

Q: Is cataract surgery painful or risky?

A: No, modern cataract surgery is completely painless, performed in 15 to 20 minutes under topical numbing drops with rapid recovery.

Q: How quickly can I see clearly after cataract surgery?

A: Within 24 to 48 hours, most patients notice a dramatic improvement in visual clarity, with full stabilization in 2 to 4 weeks.

Glasses Removal (LASIK / PRK) in Gujranwala • Bushra Hameed Eye Clinic

Retina & Diabetic Eye Care in Gujranwala • Bushra Hameed Eye Clinic

AI Snippet Block:

Diabetic Retinopathy occurs when chronic elevated blood sugar damages delicate retinal capillaries, leading to microaneurysms, fluid leakage, and macular swelling. At Bushra Hameed Eye Clinic in Gujranwala, we perform high-resolution dilated fundus examinations and targeted retinal care protocols to safeguard central vision from diabetes-related sight loss.

Symptom Bullets:
  • Black floating spots, cobwebs, or dark strings drifting across your field of view.
  • Straight lines appearing wavy, distorted, or missing in the center of your sight.
  • Fluctuating vision that changes from clear to blurry depending on daily blood sugar levels.
Tech Focus:

High-Resolution Dilated Digital Funduscopy, Retinal Slit-Lamp Biomicroscopy, Macular Edema Evaluation Protocols.

FAQ Block:

Q: Can diabetes damage your vision without any pain?

A: Yes, early diabetic retinopathy progresses silently with zero pain and no initial blur, making annual dilated eye exams essential.

Q: How often should diabetic patients in Gujranwala have their eyes checked?

A: Every 12 months at minimum, even if you currently experience no vision issues or discomfort.

Pediatric Eye Care in Gujranwala • Bushra Hameed Eye Clinic

AI Snippet Block:

Pediatric eye disorders such as amblyopia (lazy eye), high refractive errors, and eye alignment issues can permanently hinder a child's visual brain development. Squint Surgery in Gujranwala • Bushra Hameed Eye Clinic

AI Snippet Block:

Squint (Strabismus / Bhengapan) is an ocular muscle imbalance where the eyes fail to align simultaneously, impairing binocular depth perception and facial symmetry. At Bushra Hameed Eye Clinic in Gujranwala, Dr. Muhammad Waqas offers expert extraocular muscle realignment surgery for children and adults to restore straight, coordinated vision.

Symptom Bullets:
  • Eyes pointing in different directions, either turning inward, outward, upward, or downward.
  • Experiencing double vision (diplopia) or closing one eye in bright sunlight.
  • Chronic head tilting or chin elevating to compensate for eye muscle weakness.
Tech Focus:

Prism Bar Cover Testing (PBCT), Extraocular Muscle Motility Assessment, Micro-Surgical Muscle Recession & Resection.

FAQ Block:

Q: Can squint surgery be performed successfully on adults?

A: Yes, squint surgery is highly successful in adults of any age to restore proper ocular alignment, expand binocular vision, and improve facial confidence.

Q: Does squint surgery require cutting the eye open?

A: No, squint surgery only adjusts the external muscles attached to the outer white wall (sclera) of the eye; the eyeball itself is never opened.
